Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 19, 2026 — AI-assisted legal review; not reviewed by a licensed attorney

1. Who We Are

Movie Snob is a solo-developer project, developed and operated by an independent developer. This policy explains what information Movie Snob collects, how it's used, and your choices. Where this policy says "we" or "us," it means the developer of Movie Snob.

2. Information We Collect

Account information (optional). Movie Snob offers Sign in with Apple to enable cross-device sync. This is optional — you can skip it and use the app fully without signing in. If you do sign in, Apple shares your name and/or email with us (or a private relay address, if you choose to hide your email from us). This information is stored only on your device (in local app storage) and is never sent to our servers, our analytics tool, or any third party. Movie data you create. Your movie logs, ratings, reviews, and watchlist are information you generate by using the app. Location (optional, one-time use). If you choose to let Movie Snob find current box-office movies playing near you, we ask iOS for your approximate "when in use" location and convert it into a two-letter country code (e.g. "US") using Apple's own on-device/Apple-server geocoding. We only use and store that resulting country code, never your precise coordinates — see Section 6. Anonymous usage data. We use PostHog, a product analytics tool, to understand how the app is used — for example, which screens are viewed or which recommendations get watched. This is tied to a random, anonymous ID that PostHog generates, never to your name, email, Apple ID, or any other personal identifier. We do not use session recording or screen replay. We do not use Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA) or any ad-tracking technology for analytics purposes. Advertising data. We show non-personalized ads through Google AdMob. See Section 5 for what that involves.

3. Where Your Data Lives

By default, your data stays on your device. Movie Snob does not operate its own account system or its own server-side database of your movie data. While sync is enabled, your ratings, watchlist, reviews, and favorite movie sync via Apple's CloudKit to your own private iCloud account. This is Apple's private, per-user storage system — we don't have access to this data, and it isn't stored on any server we control. Sync is available to everyone at no cost right now; if that ever changes to a paid feature, this policy will be updated to reflect it. If sync isn't enabled for your account, your data stays local to your device only and does not sync anywhere.

We don't sell your data, and we don't share it with data brokers. We do show non-personalized ads through Google AdMob, described in Section 5 — those ads are not personalized to you, and we don't use IDFA-based ad tracking of any kind.

5. Advertising

Movie Snob shows native, in-list advertisements through Google AdMob to help support free access to the app. Every ad request the app makes is explicitly marked non-personalized — we never request IDFA-based ad personalization, and the app never shows an App Tracking Transparency prompt, because it isn't doing IDFA-based tracking in the first place. Even for non-personalized ads, Google (as an independent data controller for its advertising service) may process some technical information to serve ads, measure their performance, and prevent fraud — this can include your device type, IP address and the general (non-precise) location it implies, the app you're using, and coarse ad-interaction data (like whether an ad loaded or was shown). Movie Snob also includes Apple's SKAdNetwork identifiers, a privacy-preserving install-attribution mechanism Apple provides so ad networks can measure ad effectiveness without tracking individual users. We don't control what Google or its ad partners do with the technical data their systems collect, and their own privacy practices apply to that processing — see Google's privacy policy for details. If you'd rather not see ads at all, the only way to do that today is to stop using the app; an ad-free option may be offered as part of a future paid tier, and this policy will be updated if so.

6. Location Information

Movie Snob can optionally use your location to show current box-office movies playing near you. If you allow this, iOS asks for "when in use" location access, and we use Apple's Core Location and geocoding services to convert your location into a two-letter country code — nothing more precise than that is stored or used. That country code is then sent to TMDB (through our own server proxy) to filter box-office results for your region. We never store your precise coordinates, and we never send them to TMDB, PostHog, AdMob, or anyone else. This is entirely optional: you can decline the location prompt (or turn it off later in iOS Settings) and pick your country manually instead, with no loss of any other app functionality.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Data stored only on your device is deleted when you delete the app from your device. Data synced via CloudKit lives in your own iCloud account. Deleting the Movie Snob app from your device does not automatically delete your synced iCloud data — this is standard behavior for iCloud-based apps. You can manage or delete this data through your Apple iCloud account settings. Anonymous PostHog analytics events are not tied to your identity, so there isn't a way for us to identify and delete one specific person's event history. If this matters to you, you can decline to use PostHog-affected features, or contact us with questions. We don't retain your precise location at all — see Section 6. A dedicated in-app "Export My Data" and account-deletion feature is planned but not yet built.

8. Children's Privacy

Movie Snob is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with information, please contact us so we can remove it.

9. Your Choices

• You can use Movie Snob fully without signing in. • You can decline sync at any time; your data will remain local to your device. • You can decline location access and pick your country manually instead; you can also change this later in iOS Settings. • You can delete the app at any time to remove locally stored data. • You can manage or cancel your subscription anytime in your iOS Settings, once a paid subscription exists.

10. Your Privacy Rights (California, EU/UK, and Elsewhere)

If you're a California resident, the CCPA gives you the right to know the categories of personal information we collect and why, and generally the right to request deletion of personal information we hold about you, and the right not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights. Based on what's described in this policy, the categories of information Movie Snob collects and their purposes are: identifiers (an optional name/email from Sign in with Apple, kept only on your device), your own user-generated content (movie logs, ratings, watchlist), approximate location (used once, on-device, to derive a country code — see Section 6), and internet/device activity (anonymous PostHog analytics events, and technical data AdMob's non-personalized ads may process — see Sections 2 and 5). We do not sell personal information and do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, so there's no "opt out of sale/sharing" mechanism to offer. To ask a question about your data or request that we act on it, email moviesnob@icloud.com. If you're in the EU, UK, or another jurisdiction with its own data-protection law, please understand that Movie Snob is built and operated by a solo, independent developer with no dedicated legal or compliance team and no EU or UK legal representative appointed at this time. We've tried to build the app in a genuinely privacy-protective way — minimal data collection, local-first storage, anonymous analytics, non-personalized advertising — but we're not making a claim of full GDPR/UK GDPR compliance as a formal program. If you're located in one of these regions and have questions or requests about your data, email us at moviesnob@icloud.com and we'll do our best to respond consistent with the spirit of these laws; if the app develops meaningful usage from these regions, this section should be revisited with a licensed attorney familiar with that framework.

11. Security

We rely on Apple's built-in security for on-device storage, iCloud/CloudKit, and Sign in with Apple. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, but we don't operate a separate server-side database of your personal movie data that could itself be a target.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Continued use of Movie Snob after a change means you accept the revised policy.
