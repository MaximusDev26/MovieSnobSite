Support

Zero apologies. All opinions.

Movie Snob is a personal movie diary — log what you watch, rate it your way, and build a ranked collection that's actually yours. It's built and maintained by a solo, independent developer.

Need help, found a bug, or have feedback?

The fastest way to reach a real person is email — every message goes directly to the developer, no support ticket queue.

Email moviesnob@icloud.com

Feature requests

Movie Snob has a built-in Feature Wishlist — open the app, go to Settings, and look for the Feature Wishlist hub to submit ideas, upvote others', and see the current roadmap and release notes.

Common questions

Is my data private? Yes — by default everything stays on your device. If you turn on sync, your data goes to your own private iCloud account via Apple's CloudKit, never to a server the developer controls. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Does the app use ads? Yes, non-personalized ads help support free access to the app. No ad is ever targeted using your personal data or Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA).

Where does movie data come from? The Movie Database (TMDB). This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.

Movie Snob is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or certified by TMDB, JustWatch, or any streaming service.
