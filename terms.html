5. Your Data, and the One Server We Run

Movie Snob does not operate a user-account system, and we do not keep a database of your movie logs, ratings, or watchlist on any server we control. That data lives on your device and, if you choose to sign in and turn on sync, in your own private iCloud account through Apple's CloudKit. We cannot see your iCloud data.

We do run one small server. It acts as a relay for movie-information requests, so that our access credentials for third-party movie data are not exposed inside the App itself. When your device looks up a movie, the relay receives that request and your device's IP address, passes the request along, and returns the result. It does not receive your name, your email, your Apple ID, or your movie logs, ratings, or watchlist. Our Privacy Policy, Section 4, explains what the relay logs and for how long.

You are responsible for keeping your device, Apple ID, and iCloud account secure, and for what happens if you do not. We are not responsible for data loss caused by your device, your iCloud storage limits, Apple's services, or other factors outside our control.
